## Design Context

### Users
Students tracking study time for exams and coursework. Used in study sessions, libraries, and at home for focused study work.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, utilitarian, clean. Gets the job done without distraction.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimal design with light/dark mode toggle. Focus on function over form. Subtle polish where it aids usability.

### Design Principles
1. Content-first: UI recedes, subjects and time data are the focus
2. Information-dense but scannable: compact display fits many subjects
3. Fast interactions: instant feedback, no unnecessary animations
4. Both themes fully supported: dark mode not an afterthought
